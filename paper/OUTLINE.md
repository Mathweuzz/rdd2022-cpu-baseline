# Paper outline — CPU-budgeted multi-domain road damage detection

## Working title

**Compute-Constrained Road Damage Detection across Geographic and Sensor Domains**

## Contributions to validate

1. A sequence-leakage-aware cleaning and evaluation protocol for RDD2022.
2. A deterministic, equal-domain training subset with a fixed CPU budget.
3. Domain-, class-, scale-, and negative-image-aware evaluation.
4. A reproducible compact baseline and an explicit account of the accuracy cost
   of CPU-only training.

## Planned figures

- Dataset composition, classes, and bounding-box scales.
- Domain-sliced test performance.
- Training-budget and model-cost comparison.
- Qualitative successes and failures by domain.

## Planned tables

- Dataset and split statistics.
- Main detector comparison.
- AP by class and domain.
- Negative-image and sampling ablations.
